# Research Citation


## When to use

Use for facts that may be current, niche, legal, financial, medical, technical-version-specific, or source-sensitive.

## Inputs to check

- exact question
- date sensitivity
- source type needed
- required citation style
- uncertainty level

## Workflow

1. Identify whether current sources are needed.
2. Prefer primary sources.
3. Compare multiple sources when useful.
4. Mark claims as Certain, Likely, Possible, or Guessing.
5. Cite important claims.
6. Separate facts from recommendations.

## Output format

- Answer
- Evidence
- Risks/caveats
- Recommendation

## Quality checklist

- [ ] No invented facts
- [ ] Current facts verified
- [ ] Important claims cited
- [ ] Uncertainty labeled
- [ ] Conflicting evidence noted

## Stop conditions

Stop if reliable evidence is unavailable; say what was checked and what remains unknown.
